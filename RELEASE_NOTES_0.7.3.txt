PowerPilot 0.7.3

UI polish — notification bell

- Increased the notification bell icon from 24 px to 28 px while keeping the same compact 42 px button.
- Added a tiny additional hover scale (up to ~30 px) without changing the hit box.
- Added a short notification "ring burst": eight small golden particles travel outward inside the button and fade away.
- The burst starts on first hover-entry and when a new unread notification arrives.
- Added a subtle damped bell wiggle during the burst.
- Effects are clipped strictly to the notification button, so they cannot overlap Settings or the page below.
- The effect is one-shot (~560 ms), not a permanent idle animation, to avoid unnecessary GPU/CPU work.
- Animation mode "off" disables the burst/wiggle while keeping the larger icon.
